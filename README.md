# ⚡ CutOS Master — iPhone Diet & Habit Trainer

**CutOS Master** is an elite, high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA) designed with a native iOS aesthetic (OLED dark mode, frosted glass cards, and smooth Cupertino animations) for students and bachelors managing fixed mess schedules, tight budgets, and strict fat-loss goals.

---

## 🚀 Key Master Features

* **Bachelor Mess Integration:** Pre-loaded with your exact routine (room oats/eggs, campus guava, mess egg lunch, evening snack, and mess chicken dinner).
* **Dedicated Quick-Add Auto-Estimator:** Easily log unplanned foods (cake, burgers, street snacks) on the fly with automatic calorie and protein estimation.
* **Strict Time & Watchdog Alarms:** Features an 11:00 PM kitchen lock and late-caffeine guardrails to protect your sleep and metabolic recovery.
* **Caffeine & Hydration Tracking:** Timestamp monitor for coffee/tea and 2.5L water glass counter.
* **Local Persistence:** Automatically saves your logs and 7-day streak locally in your phone's browser storage.
* **iOS Optimized:** Full-screen standalone PWA mode with custom SVG app icon and dynamic safe-area padding.

---

## 📱 How to Install on Your iPhone

1. Open your hosted GitHub Pages URL in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button in the bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**—CutOS Master will now launch like a native application directly from your home screen!
