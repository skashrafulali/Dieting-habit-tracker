# ⚡ CutOS — iPhone Diet & Habit Assistant

**CutOS** is a hyper-minimal, lightning-fast Progressive Web App (PWA) built specifically for students and bachelors managing fixed mess meals, tight budgets, and campus schedules while trying to cut belly fat.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Bachelor Mess Sync:** Pre-loaded with your fixed routine (room oats/eggs, campus guava, mess egg lunch, evening snack, and mess chicken dinner).
* **Smart Campus Swaps:** Log campus snacks (*peyaju*, *alur chop*, *khichuri*) on the fly. The app automatically recalculates your remaining daily calorie deficit (~1,650 kcal target) and adapts your night mess meal strategy.
* **Caffeine & Dudh Cha Timestamp Monitor:** Track black coffee, milk coffee, Coffee-mate mix, or campus *dudh cha* with exact timestamps and late-night sleep warnings.
* **Hydration & Chia Tracker:** One-tap 250ml water glass counter and daily soaked chia seed bottle toggle.
* **Local Persistence:** Automatically saves your 7-day streak and daily logs locally in your browser (`localStorage`).
* **iOS Optimized:** Full-screen standalone PWA mode with custom app icon, dynamic safe-area padding (for notch/Dynamic Island), and touch-friendly bottom sheets.

---

## 📱 How to Install on Your iPhone

1. Open your hosted GitHub Pages URL in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button in the bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**—CutOS will now launch like a native app from your home screen!

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Single-file HTML5 / Vanilla JavaScript (No heavy build steps or frameworks)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (via CDN)
* **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
